# Getting to Know Lily

Answer in your own words. Short answers, incomplete thoughts, or "skip" are all fine. Only facts you approve for public use will be added to the portfolio assistant.

## The Person

1. Outside engineering, what are two or three things you genuinely enjoy? What does a good free weekend look like?
2. What foods or cuisines do you love? Any go-to meal, drink, or snack?
3. What is a small, unexpected fact about you that you would enjoy sharing with a visitor?

## The Engineer

4. What first got you interested in engineering? What keeps you interested now?
5. Which project are you proudest of, and why? What part was specifically yours?
6. Tell me about a technical problem that was genuinely difficult. What did you try, and what finally worked?
7. What would a teammate say it is like to work with you? A real example helps more than a list of adjectives.
8. What are you learning or experimenting with lately?

## What's Next

9. What kinds of roles, teams, or problems excite you? Is there anything you are actively looking for that the assistant may mention publicly?
10. What do you want a recruiter to remember after a two-minute conversation with the assistant?

## Voice and Boundaries

11. Should it sound friendly and lightly witty, warm and professional, or casual and direct? Share a sentence you would actually say, and one you would never say.
12. What should stay private? Which questions should it always redirect to you, such as availability, compensation, personal contact details, or anything else?

Your answers become approved context, not an automatic training upload. Visitors cannot change your profile by telling the assistant new "facts" about you.

## Professional Follow-Ups

These are the remaining questions sent together after the initial interview. Skip anything that should not be public. Your Sidekick origin story, RL challenge, food and music preferences, career interests, and preferred tone are already recorded.

1. At Shopify, what did you enjoy most about the work, and why? A specific moment involving Sidekick, POS, merchants, or teammates would help.
2. Why did you pursue the Meta Engineering Fellowship? What did you discover you liked or disliked about infrastructure and reliability work?
3. At AGF, how did you find the reporting or automation problems worth solving? What was personally satisfying about that experience?
4. Why did you start AsterFind? Was there a personal frustration or curiosity behind it, and which part of building it did you enjoy most?
5. What drew you to Temporal Maze, and why did you decide to take it from Python into Unreal Engine? What part felt most creatively or technically satisfying?
6. Why did you build Ecoland? What question did you want to explore, and what behavior or result surprised you most?
7. Which research project did you enjoy most, and why? How has doing experimental physics influenced the way you now build software?
8. When you enter an unfamiliar area, such as RL, how do you actually learn it? Walk through your first few days rather than giving an idealized process.
9. Describe a real engineering tradeoff or a time you changed your mind. What alternatives did you consider, and what made you choose the final approach?
10. What is it like to collaborate with you? Share a moment involving feedback, disagreement, helping a teammate, or asking for help.
11. What kind of work energizes you, and what drains you? What team environment helps you do your best work?
12. Are any company details, project stories, personal topics, or opinions off-limits for the public assistant? Which questions should it always redirect to you?
